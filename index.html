Questions?
We're here to help.

Know what to expect before you begin.

Do I need a credit card for the Sleep free trial?

No. You can try Sleep for seven days without providing a payment card.

Will I be charged automatically after the Sleep trial?

No. You must actively choose to start a paid subscription.

What does Care include?

Two 45-minute video sessions per month with a licensed psychotherapist and weekday chat support.

Can I request a refund for Care?

Yes. The proposed Care plan includes a money-back guarantee upon request within 30 days.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Once a paid subscription starts, it renews monthly until cancelled. The renewal terms must be clearly shown before payment.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. In this demonstration, open subscription settings, select Cancel subscription, then confirm. The example ends access at the end of the current billing month, with no further renewal. This is a demonstration, not a real customer account.
